Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 06, 2026

Job Tracker respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Demo Application

Job Tracker is a demonstration application and is not intended for production use. Please use test data only. Do not enter real personal, confidential, or sensitive information.

Information We Handle

We collect only the information needed to provide the Service:

  • your username and password
  • job application information that you choose to save
  • basic information needed to keep your account signed in and secure

How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • create and maintain your account
  • save and display your job applications
  • keep the Service secure and working properly
  • comply with legal obligations

Sign-in Information

Job Tracker uses essential browser storage and cookies to keep you signed in and protect your account. These are not used for advertising or tracking.

Analytics and Marketing

We do not use your information for advertising or marketing. Job Tracker does not use analytics or web beacons.

Visibility and Sharing

Your account and job application information are private and are not publicly visible through the Service.

We do not sell your personal information or share your job application information with other users. We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect the Service and its users.

Retention

We keep your information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, protect your account, and meet legal obligations.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account through the Service. When you delete your account, your account and associated job application information are deleted. Information may be retained when required by law or needed to resolve disputes or protect the Service. If you lost access to your account, you can request account deletion at info@ywesseloh.com.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. Contact us at info@ywesseloh.com to make a request or ask a privacy question.

Children

The Service is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post changes on this page and update the "Last updated" date.
